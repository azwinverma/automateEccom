# Product Vision & PRD

## Vision
Build the operating system that lets online and social-commerce sellers run their entire business from one place while AI and automation handle repetitive work.

## Core promise
**Capture or select a product once. The platform prepares, publishes, sells, fulfills, supports and learns from it across connected channels.**

## Primary users
### Seller-founder
1–5 staff, 50–2,000 SKUs, social DMs, spreadsheets, COD and couriers.

### Ecommerce operator
Manages catalog, stock, messages, promotions, shipments, returns and reports.

### Multi-store owner
Needs multiple stores, warehouses, users, permissions, analytics and APIs.

### Operations staff
Need clear queues for messages, packing, courier exceptions, returns and refunds.

## Jobs to be done
- turn photos into sellable content
- publish once across channels
- synchronize product/price/inventory
- auto-answer repetitive questions
- turn chats into orders
- collect/record payment
- create shipments
- track delivery and COD settlements
- handle returns/exchanges/refunds
- create images, reels and premium AI video
- calculate real product profit
- re-engage customers
- surface only exceptions

## Product modes
### Assisted
AI prepares; merchant approves.

### Smart
Low-risk tasks auto-run; important tasks require approval.

### Autopilot
Maximum allowed automation within seller-defined policies, budgets and platform restrictions.

## North-star metric
**Automated order lifecycle completion rate**:
eligible orders completed from inquiry/order creation through delivery without merchant manual intervention.

Supporting metrics:
- product upload → publish time
- AI customer resolution rate
- chat-to-order conversion
- publish success
- stock sync accuracy
- fulfillment automation
- return automation
- exception rate
- merchant weekly active usage
- GMV processed
- provider cost as % of subscription revenue

## Non-goals initially
- consumer marketplace
- lending/banking
- proprietary frontier model training
- every global channel on day one
- full enterprise ERP
