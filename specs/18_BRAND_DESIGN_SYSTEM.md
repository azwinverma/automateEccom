# Brand & Design System

## Brand character
Joyful, fast, capable, warm, human, trustworthy, modern, commerce-focused.

## Core experience
**Human-crafted commerce software with AI inside — not AI aesthetics pasted on top.**

## Color balance
~70% neutral surfaces, 20% primary/soft brand, 10% vivid accent.

Suggested palette:
- Primary Blue #2563EB
- Indigo #4F46E5
- Fresh Teal #14B8A6
- Joy Coral #FF6B6B
- Sun Yellow #FBBF24
- Ink #0F172A
- Slate #475569
- Cloud #F8FAFC
- White #FFFFFF

Semantic success/warning/danger/info are separate design tokens.

## Typography
English: Inter.
Nepali: Noto Sans Devanagari.
Readable, not “futuristic AI”.

## Signature components
- Product Brain Card
- Channel Sync Rail
- Autopilot Status Strip
- Exception Inbox Card
- Order Journey
- AI Action Timeline
- Product Profit Card
- Video Credit Meter
- Inventory Risk Meter
- Conversation-to-Order Card
- Automation Recipe Card

## Anti-generic rules
Do not use:
- purple/blue gradient everywhere
- cards around every section
- giant pill controls
- sparkles as generic AI branding
- random 3D blobs/clay characters
- fake logos/testimonials
- repeated generic SaaS landing-page formula
- vague “unlock/revolutionize/supercharge” copy

## Photography
Real sellers, products, shelves, packaging, couriers, studios, shops and warehouses.
No staged handshake/high-five stock.

## Marketing site
Show real lifecycle: add product → AI assets → publish → customer conversation →
order → delivery → profit/retention.

## Web app
Operational pages calm and neutral. Color reserved for action, risk, automation and growth.

## Flutter
Warmer, faster, clear capture/upload, progress, exceptions, orders and content preview.

## Motion
150ms fast, ~220ms standard, ~320ms large. No decorative infinite motion.

## Mandatory design process
brief → wireframe → 2 considered layout directions for major surfaces →
component review → build → responsive QA → accessibility QA → human review.

## Acceptance test
If the logo were removed, the interface should still feel recognizably like this commerce product.
