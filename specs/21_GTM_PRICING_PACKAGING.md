## Value metric
Value is shown through measurable outcomes in-product:
- Autopilot Score
- hours saved
- recommendation impact
- return-rate reduction

Upgrade prompts reference the merchant's own data (08 §Autopilot-aware upgrade suggestions).

## Rules
- No fake numbers, testimonials or logos.
- Marketing claims must match an in-product metric.
